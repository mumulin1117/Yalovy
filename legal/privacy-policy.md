# Yalovy Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Yalovy is a pet-focused pet-circle app for saving and sharing pet moments, including photos, short notes, daily routines, grooming, training progress, seasonal looks, and selected extended pet videos. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

If you have questions or privacy requests, contact us at yalovy@gmail.com.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the information needed to provide Yalovy:

- Account information: email address, username, display name, profile details, account status, login state, and account deletion status.
- User content: pet photos, pet videos, captions, notes, tags, comments, reactions, saved posts, and other content you choose to create or publish.
- Pet Circle and safety information: reports, blocked users, moderation decisions, user interactions, follow relationships, and content visibility settings.
- Acquisition-related information: token total, token package selections, transaction history, and records needed to unlock or publish optional paid video content. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not receive your full card number or checkout credentials.
- Device and usage information: app version, device type, language, app events, local preferences, and basic diagnostic information used to operate, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
- Permission-based media access: camera, photo library, and microphone access are used only when you choose to take, select, record, upload, or publish pet content.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Create, authenticate, maintain, and delete accounts.
- Display your profile, pet moments, posts, comments, reactions, saved items, and pet-circle feed.
- Support photo posting, optional paid video posting, token balances, and acquisition history.
- Provide safety tools, including report, block, moderation review, and abuse prevention.
- Enforce our Terms of Service and pet-circle rules.
- Respond to support, privacy, safety, and account requests.
- Maintain app security, prevent spam, troubleshoot issues, and improve app reliability.

## 3. User Content and Visibility

Content you publish to the pet-circle feed may be visible to other users. Content saved privately or kept as an unpublished draft remains available only within your account or device unless you choose to publish it.

You are responsible for the content you upload. Do not post content that is illegal, abusive, hateful, explicit, harassing, spam, fraudulent, infringing, or unrelated to a pet-focused pet-circle.

## 4. Reports, Blocking, and Moderation

Yalovy includes tools to report content or users and to block unwanted interactions. Reported content, account identifiers, reasons, and related context may be reviewed to decide whether content should be removed, visibility should be limited, or an account should be suspended or terminated.

Blocking a user limits interactions between you and that user inside the app. Reports and blocks may be retained as safety records to help prevent repeat abuse.

## 5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information only when necessary:

- With service providers that help operate, host, secure, analyze, or support the app.
- With Apple for App Store purchases, refunds, checkout processing, and receipt validation.
- When required by law, legal process, or enforceable government request.
- To protect users, Yalovy, or the public from fraud, abuse, security threats, or rights violations.
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of app operations, subject to continued privacy protections.

## 6. App Store Purchases and Tokens

Optional paid content and token-related features are handled through the Apple App Store where applicable. Apple processes checkout information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. Yalovy receives acquisition confirmation, product identifiers, transaction status, and related records needed to provide the purchased app feature.

Tokens are an in-app virtual item used only within Yalovy. They have no cash value, are not transferable outside the app, and are subject to the App Store acquisition rules.

## 7. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including account operation, user content display, safety review, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and backup recovery.

When you delete content, it is removed from normal app views. Some copies may remain for a limited time in backups, logs, moderation records, or legal records where necessary.

## 8. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion from within the app where the Delete Account option is available, or by contacting yalovy@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

When an account is deleted, we remove or de-identify the account profile and login information, and we stop displaying the account as an active user. We may retain limited records if needed for legal compliance, acquisition records, fraud prevention, chargeback handling, safety enforcement, or moderation history.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain uses of your personal information. You may also:

- Edit your profile information.
- Delete posts you created.
- Block users.
- Report inappropriate users or content.
- Request account deletion.
- Contact us about privacy or safety concerns.

Send requests to yalovy@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

## 10. Children

Yalovy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact yalovy@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so you should use a strong password and keep your account credentials private.

## 12. International Use

Your information may be processed in the country where you live and in other locations where we or our service providers operate. We take steps designed to protect information consistently with this Policy.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will provide notice in the app or by another reasonable method. Continued use of Yalovy after an update means the updated Policy applies.
