* **App Store 描述：
A first walk. A new trick. A favorite sleeping corner. The tiny habits that change as a pet grows.
Yalovy gives these moments a place to stay. Share photos with short notes, record everyday routines, and collect stories about playtime, grooming, training progress, seasonal looks, and unexpected discoveries. 
The community feed stays centered on pets. Browse daily records from other owners, discover different routines and personalities, and exchange thoughtful reactions around the moments being shared. 
Creators may also publish selected extended pet videos as optional paid content. While regular photo-and-text sharing remains available as part of the community experience.
Users control what they publish and can manage unwanted interactions at any time. Reporting, blocking, and content moderation tools are available to address inappropriate posts or abusive behavior and help maintain a respectful, pet-focused environment.
